# Team Hauchdev Documentation

Welcome to the official documentation for **Team Hauchdev**. Here you will find everything you need to install, configure, extend, and contribute to our Minecraft plugins.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="hLobby" icon="server" href="/en/hLobby">
    Modern lobby management for Velocity proxies. Smart routing, teleport countdowns, kick redirection, multi-language support, and a public API.
  </Card>
  <Card title="hChat" icon="comments" href="/en/hChat">
    A complete chat system for Paper 1.20+ servers. Channels, direct messages, moderation, filters, AFK, mail, placeholders, and more.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
