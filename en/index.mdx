Welcome to the official documentation for **Team Hauchdev**. Here you will find everything you need to install, configure, extend, and contribute to our Minecraft plugins.

<CardGroup cols={2}>

## What you can do here

- **Get started quickly** with step-by-step installation guides.
- **Configure every feature** using full config references and examples.
- **Integrate** with PlaceholderAPI, DiscordSRV, Velocity, LuckPerms, and Vault.
- **Build on top** of our public APIs for hLobby and hChat.
- **Contribute** code, translations, bug reports, or ideas.
