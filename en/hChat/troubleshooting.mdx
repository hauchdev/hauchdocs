## Chat format does not show colors

- Make sure `chat.format-mode` is set correctly (`legacy`, `minimessage`, or `auto`).
- If using PlaceholderAPI expansions, download and enable them.
- Verify the player has the correct format permission (e.g., `hchat.format.vip`).

## Players cannot send private messages

- Verify `hchat.message` and `hchat.reply` are granted.
- If the target is offline, mail is stored only if the target has played before.
- If the sender has DND enabled, they cannot send or receive DMs unless configured otherwise.

## Staff chat is visible to everyone

Check that the `staff` channel has proper `speak-permission` and `see-permission` set, and that non-staff players do not have those permissions.

## Filters are too aggressive or not working

- Enable `general.debug` to see filter decisions in the console.
- Adjust `max-uppercase-percent`, `similarity-threshold`, and pattern lists.
- Add false positives to the anti-ad whitelist.

## PlaceholderAPI placeholders show as raw text

- Make sure PlaceholderAPI is installed and the required expansions are loaded.
- Some placeholders require the target player to be online.

## AFK not detected automatically

Check `afk.auto-timeout-seconds` and `afk.unset-on-activity`. If the timeout is too high or activity detection is disabled, AFK will not trigger.

## Still stuck?

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/hauchdev/hChat/issues) with:

- Your Paper version.
- Your hChat version.
- Your `config.yml` (redact sensitive data).
- Relevant console logs.
